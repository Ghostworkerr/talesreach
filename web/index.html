Talesreach

A free browser MMORPG — no download. Step into the Crossroads Inn and live a life in a medieval fantasy world, on your phone or your PC.

▶ Play now — it's free

Works in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. Turn your phone sideways for the best view. Players must be 13 or older.

On iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen to install.

Live a life, not just a level

You log in and you're someone. The innkeeper knows your name, the bard takes your requests, and every way of living is a real way to play.

⚔ Adventure

Explore forests and ruins, fight monsters with real-time action combat, and earn bounties and titles.

🌾 Farm & craft

Grow crops, raise animals, cook, brew and forge — and sell what you make to other players.

🍺 Make friends

Meet people at the inn, chat, form a party, rent a room and wake up by the fire.

🌙 A living world

Days pass and seasons turn. The inn staff keep their own routines, and the bard plays every evening.

📱 Phone or PC

Drag to walk or tap where to go on a phone; use WASD or click on a computer. Play together across devices.

🛡 Safe & fair

No pay-to-win. Chat filters, mute, block and report keep the realm friendly. See the rules.
