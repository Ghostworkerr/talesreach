Privacy policy

The short version: we keep only what the game needs to work and to keep players safe. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your information.

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

What we store on our server

WhatWhy
Your date of birthTo apply the age rules (13+ only; extra protection for players under 18). It is never shown to anyone.
A login key for each of your devicesSo you stay signed in. We only keep a scrambled (one-way) version of it.
Your save code, if you make oneSo you can play on another device. Again, only a scrambled version is kept.
Your character's name and look, coins, inn room and chestTo run your character.
Who you have blockedSo blocked players can't reach you.
Your last few chat lines (kept briefly in memory)If someone is reported, their recent chat is attached to the report as evidence.
ReportsSo moderators can act on them. A report contains the reason, an optional note, and the recent chat lines.
A record of coin changesTo keep the game economy fair and stop cheating.

We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or location.

What is kept on your device

Your browser remembers your login key, your date of birth and character (so you don't have to enter them again), your sound settings and who you've muted. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

What we never do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking on this site.
  • No analytics or tracking cookies.
  • We never sell or rent your information to anyone.

Children

Talesreach is for players aged 13 and over. If the date of birth shows someone is under 13, no account is created. Players aged 13–17 get extra protections in chat. If you believe a child under 13 is playing, please report them in the game.

Who helps us run the game

The website is hosted by Cloudflare, the game server by Render, and (once connected) the database by a hosting provider such as Neon. They process data only to run the service.

Your choices

You can stop playing at any time. To ask for your account and data to be deleted, or for any privacy question, contact the Talesreach team (a contact address will be published here soon).
