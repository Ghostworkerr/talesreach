The rules of the realm

Talesreach is a shared world. These rules keep it fun and safe for everyone — please read them before you play.

Who can play

  • You must be 13 or older.
  • Players under 18 have extra protections: phone numbers, emails, social media handles and links are removed from anything they send or see.

Be kind

  • No bullying, harassment, threats or hate speech — about anyone, for any reason.
  • No sexual content, and no sexual or romantic talk aimed at real people. In-game friendships and weddings are wholesome.
  • No seeking real-life dating or meetings with other players.
  • Disagree politely. If someone bothers you, mute or block them — it works instantly.

Keep personal details private

  • Never share your real name, address, school, phone number, passwords or photos — and never ask others for theirs.
  • Never share your save code. Anyone with it can play your character.

Play fair

  • No cheating, hacking, bots, or exploiting bugs. If you find a bug, report it instead.
  • No buying or selling coins, items or characters for real money. Talesreach never sells anything that makes you stronger.
  • Crime is part of the story in some places (bandits, bounties) — but only where the game allows it.

Names

  • Character names must be friendly: no rude words, real people's names used to mock them, or contact details.

Reporting

Tap or click another player and choose Report. Pick what happened and add a note if you like. The game attaches their recent chat so a moderator can see exactly what was said. They won't know who reported them. Reporting someone also hides them from you straight away.

What happens if rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is: a warning, a chat mute, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban. Some things — like harming or targeting young players — mean an immediate permanent ban.
